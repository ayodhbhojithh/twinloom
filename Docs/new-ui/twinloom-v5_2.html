FAQs

The rules this run-through follows, and the questions people actually ask.

Built

Before the questions people ask about a build, here are the thirteen rules the run-through itself follows. They are written down because a rule you can read is a rule you can hold us to.

1

Three layers, and no more. A list of options. Detail behind one of them. And, only in the shop, the back of the shop.

2

An area can be left alone. Picking nothing is an answer. We say what we assumed instead, rather than blocking your way forward.

3

Every open list ends the same way. "Something else — tell us in your own words", captured word for word and never rewritten into our language.

4

Open lists and closed lists. A list is open when the answer is about your business, and you may add to it. It is closed when the answer is about our build.

5

What you add carries no weight. A row you write yourself never puts a page on the sitemap by itself. It goes on the list of things to talk about, in your words.

6

An option opens detail only when it has to. Only when a further answer changes the build, the sitemap, or what we have to ask you for. Symmetry is not a reason.

7

One box per layer, not one per option. The exception is an answer that is honestly a file or a link. Those carry their own attach.

8

Everything attached lands in the side panel. Tied to the answer it came from. Anything with nothing to tie it to sits under General.

9

Unanswered means assumed, and it says so. Line by line, in the document. Touching a question makes it answered.

10

One home. Where two answers imply the same screen, the first keeps it and the second says where it went.

11

Nothing on screen is a number we price with. No days, no scores, no levels, no tiers. Questions, and a growing description of your own site.

12

Every layer ends in the same fork. Use this scope, or give more detail. Both are finished states.

13

The question asks the fact. The detail asks the consequences. Where the same fact would reach us twice, it is asked once, and the second place says where it went.

Framework note

Area one carries the real eight groups, and picking here is the same as picking on the cards. The other six carry rows I have drafted so the pattern can be judged with something in it. Those rows are provisional and are yours to cut, rename or reorder, and only area one feeds the site description so far. Four card shapes are in use: the full card where a row becomes its own way in, a wide card where you would answer in a sentence, a small card where you either have the thing or you do not, and a single row where there is nothing to compare it against.

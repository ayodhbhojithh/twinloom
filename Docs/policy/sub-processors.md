## What is needed to complete this page, not for publication

For each provider: **the name**, **what you use it for**, and **the country or region where it processes data**.

If a provider processes data outside the UK, we also need the transfer safeguard. That is usually UK adequacy regulations, the International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses. Most large providers publish this in their own data processing agreement and it can be lifted straight across.

Two consistency checks before publishing:

- The analytics provider named here must match the analytics row on the Cookies page.
- The scheduling and video-call providers named here are the ones referred to in the Privacy notice section about meetings, which also needs a retention period for meeting notes.

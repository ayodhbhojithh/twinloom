A-47 · Thoughts & inspiration · the full component

The panel, built

Everything the specification describes, working in one file: files with per-file notes, the ten-colour weighted palette, the full Colour Studio, sites with reasons, notes, context tagging, and the palette reflection strip. Styled against the style guide. No browser storage anywhere — the state is one object in memory, shaped like the session object it will become.

01 · The question

Is this triggered from route 2?

No. And it should not be.

The panel is route-independent. It is not a feature of the guided journey that the other two routes happen to lack — it is the capture surface that sits underneath all three, writing into one session object. Route 2 is simply the route that shows it docked beside something else.

The reason is in the first line of the specification: most people cannot describe what they want a website to look like, they can point at one. If the pointing only becomes possible after someone has committed to answering twenty questions, we have put the hardest thing first and the easiest thing behind it.

The decision, stated once

One capture object per session. Available before a route is chosen, during whichever route is chosen, and after submission until the session closes. Anything captured before a route is picked carries into that route, tagged with where it was captured. No route has a private copy.

So how does it differ by route?

Only in layout and in what sits next to it. Same component, same object, three chrome treatments.

RouteWhat the visitor seesWhat it actually is
1 · Send it to us A single page: drop your things, tell us what you like, send. The panel standing alone, full width, with a send bar. Not a different feature — a different layout of this component.
2 · Walk it through with us The panel docked to the right of the questions, open or closed, on every step. The same component, 360px, with the page moving aside for it.
3 · Just talk to us After the call is booked, a capture link by email: "anything you want to show us before we speak". The same standalone layout as route 1, opened against the session the booking created.

And the second half of the question

Yes — the visitor can capture separately and attach it to a route-based submission later. That is the point of one session object. Three cases, all of which happen:

Capture first, choose a route after
Someone arrives with a photograph of their shopfront and nothing else. They add it on the landing page. When they then pick route 2, the file is already in the session, tagged before a route was chosen.
Capture during, in place
Halfway through a question about opening hours they drop in a menu PDF. It is tagged to that step, so the plan can say which answer it belongs to.
Capture after, against the same session
They finish, then remember the competitor site they meant to show us. The resume link reopens the capture surface alone and adds it to the submission already made — which raises a change receipt, not a second submission.

What this rules out

A separate "upload your brand assets" page that lives outside the journey and has to be reconciled with it afterwards. Every version of that ends with two half-filled records and a person merging them by hand.

03 · What it holds

Five things, one object

1 · Files and screenshots
Drop, browse or paste. Every file gets a thumbnail, its name, and an "explain this file" note. png · jpg · webp · svg · pdf · docx · pptx · xlsx · txt. 20 MB each, 60 MB across the session. Rejections say what was wrong in plain words.
2 · Colours
An ordered list, maximum ten. Order sets the role. Every colour carries a weight that rebalances the rest to total 100%. Add by picker, by hex, or from the Studio.
3 · The Colour Studio
Image tray, drag/paste/screen snapshot, pixel loupe, the system eyedropper anywhere on screen, auto-extract with tick-to-keep, and a fine-tune square. Adds straight into the palette.
4 · Websites you like
A link and a reason. The reason matters more than the link — five sites tell us very little, five sites each with a sentence tell us almost everything. A link added without one is marked, not rejected.
5 · Notes
Anything else about the business, the customers, the taste.

And one thing it gives back

Once three colours exist, the panel shows the palette applied to a plain page at the weights set — a heading, a paragraph, a button, a footer. Not a design. A demonstration that the weight slider means something. Give something back before asking for more.

04 · The rules it obeys

Two governing, five integrating

RuleHow it is enforced here
Nothing is silently lostEvery addition writes a line to the live region and moves the tab count. A failed file stays in the list, marked, with a retry — it is never removed for you.
Never a modalThe panel is docked; the page takes a right margin. Only the Colour Studio is modal, because it is a tool, not the journey.
Context awareEvery file, colour and link records the step it was added on.
Visible landingThe count on the tab, the per-section counts, and the reflection strip. Trust mechanism, not decoration.
Into the submissionOne object, mapped to areas at submission, files attached rather than referenced.
Survives save and resumeThe object is serialisable as it stands. Uploads carry a storage key, not a blob.
Never blocksEvery field optional, every route reachable with the panel untouched.

Removal is the one destructive action, so it gets the undo

Remove a file, colour or link and an undo offer appears for eight seconds. Nothing else in the panel can lose work.

05 · Honest gaps

What this file still is not

This is the component, complete and working. It is not the production service. Twelve things stand between the two and they are all server-side or policy, not interaction:

Storage and privacy
Blob storage with signed uploads and automatic lifecycle deletion. EXIF and GPS stripped on upload. HEIC converted or rejected with an explanation. Virus scanning on anything that is not an image.
Abuse
Rate limiting and a spam gate on an unauthenticated endpoint that accepts 20 MB files.
Enforcement
The weight invariant, the ten-colour cap and the size caps are enforced here in the client only, which means they are not enforced.
Reconciliation
Two tabs on one session. Last write wins, but the badge has to agree.
Accessibility, partly closed
The Studio was mouse-only. The fine-tune square and hue strip now take arrow keys and the hex field is the escape hatch. The modal traps focus and returns it. The loupe remains a pointer affordance by nature.
Registration
ICO Tier-1 before the first real submission.

The retention line in the panel says three days on abandoned drafts. That number has to be true in the lifecycle rule before this goes anywhere near a stranger.

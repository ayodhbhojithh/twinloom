About us

We build websites that earn their keep.

TwinCoreTech is a UK studio focused on small and growing businesses. We make sites that help people find you, trust you and get in touch — and we look after them afterwards.

Our story

We started because too many SMEs were sold expensive sites that didn't bring in work. We do the opposite: scope honestly, build to a clear plan, and price it plainly.

What we believe

  • You own everything — the site, the domain, every account. No lock-in.
  • Plain English over jargon. You always know what you're getting and what it costs.
  • Proof over promises — we show results, not buzzwords.

How we work

Discover → Design → Build → Launch → Care. Milestone-based, weekly updates, a walkthrough at handover, and ongoing support. See our partners for SEO/AI, brand and social.

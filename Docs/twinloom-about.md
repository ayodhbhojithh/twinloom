# About TwinLoom

TwinLoom builds websites, supported by our digital and consultancy services.

TwinLoom is a trading name of TwinCoreTech Ltd, a SaaS company building custom software for businesses. If what sits behind your website needs building rather than buying, that work sits in the same group.

## Our core offering

**Websites.** From a handful of pages through to online shops, booking systems, and sites that connect to the software you already run.

**Custom software.** Managed software built for what your website sits on top of, by TwinCoreTech. Same group, same people to talk to.

Every site we build gets the same eleven inclusions, whatever its size.

- It works on a phone, a tablet and a computer
- Your visual identity, applied to layouts we already have
- Set up so search engines can find and read every page
- Secure, with access managed where it is needed
- Hosted, backed up and monitored
- Built to load quickly and stay still while it loads
- Reviewed for accessibility before it goes live
- An enquiry form that reaches the right inbox
- Analytics and Search Console, in accounts you own
- Two weeks of attention after launch
- A handover pack, and a session with the people who will use it

## Our services

Everything that is not the build itself.

Provided by us or through our specialist partners, and named in the proposal before the work starts either way. Take any of them, or none.

**Digital consultancy.** Working out what the site has to do, who it is for, and what it needs to connect to.

**Website care.** Keeping the site current once it is live, and the services that run alongside it: domain and business email, social media set-up and maintenance, and regular content.

**Brand identity.** Logo, visual identity, photography and imagery, artwork, and the words on the page.

**Digital campaign management.** Running and measuring campaigns once the site is live.
